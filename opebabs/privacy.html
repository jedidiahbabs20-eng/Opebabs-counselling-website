CONFIDENTIALITY POLICY FOR CLIENT ENGAGEMENT

THIS POLICY ENSURES THAT ALL CLIENTS' INFORMATION IS KEPT PRIVATE AND HANDLED WITH CARE AND RESPECT.
  1. INTRODUCTION:
    • WE ARE COMMITTED TO CREATING A SAFE SPACE WHERE CLIENTS CAN SPEAK FREELY. THIS POLICY OUTLINES HOW WE PROTECT CLIENT PRIVACY IN ALL MARRIAGE AND RELATIONSHIP COUNSELING SERVICES.
  2. OUR COMMITMENT:
    • WE KEEP ALL CLIENT INFORMATION CONFIDENTIAL, FOLLOWING ETHICAL AND LEGAL STANDARDS.
  3. WHO IT APPLIES TO:
    • THIS POLICY APPLIES TO ALL STAFF, INCLUDING COUNSELORS, ADMIN STAFF, AND INTERNS.
  4. WHAT ARE THE CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION?:

    5. THIS INCLUDES:

    • NAMES, ADDRESSES, CONTACT INFO
    • SESSION NOTES AND COUNSELING RECORDS
    • ASSESSMENTS AND TEST RESULTS
    • ALL CLIENT-COUNSELOR COMMUNICATION
    • ANYTHING SHARED DURING SESSIONS
  5. STORAGE AND SECURITY:
    • INFORMATION IS COLLECTED TO HELP PROVIDE BETTER COUNSELING.
    • IT IS STORED IN LOCKED CABINETS OR ENCRYPTED SYSTEMS.
    • ONLY AUTHORIZED STAFF CAN ACCESS IT
  6. WHEN INFORMATION MAY BE SHARED:
    • WE ONLY SHARE INFORMATION IF:
    • YOU GIVE WRITTEN CONSENT
    • THE LAW REQUIRES IT (E.G. RISK OF HARM, ABUSE, COURT ORDER)
  7. YOUR RIGHTS AS A CLIENT:

    8. YOU HAVE THE RIGHT TO:

    • SEE YOUR RECORDS (AS ALLOWED BY LAW)
    • REQUEST CORRECTIONS
    • EXPECT CONFIDENTIALITY, WITH FEW LEGAL EXCEPTIONS
  8. IF CONFIDENTIALITY IS BROKEN:
    • ANY BREACH WILL BE REPORTED AND HANDLED IMMEDIATELY.
    • WE WILL CONTAIN THE ISSUE
    • NOTIFY AFFECTED CLIENTS
    • REVIEW AND IMPROVE OUR PRACTICES
  9. STAFF TRAINING:
    • HIS POLICY IS REVIEWED EVERY YEAR OR WHEN LAWS CHANGE. UPDATES WILL BE SHARED AS NEEDED.
  10. REFUND POLICY:
    • CANCELING MORE THAN 24 HOURS BEFORE YOUR SESSION:– 90% REFUND (10% ADMIN FEE IS DEDUCTED)
    • CANCELING LESS THAN 24 HOURS BEFORE THE SESSION:– NO REFUND
  11. PENALTY FOR APPOINTMENT CHANCES:
    • RESCHEDULING LESS THAN 24 HOURS TO SESSION:– 5% PENALTY FROM YOUR INITIAL PAYMENT
    • NO RESCHEDULING ALLOWED WITHIN 1 HOUR TO SESSION:– SESSION IS FORFEITED OR MUST CONTINUE AS SCHEDULED
    • ARRIVING LATE:– YOU ONLY GET THE REMAINING TIME IN YOUR SESSION (NO EXTRA TIME ADDED)
