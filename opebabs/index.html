BIO

I am a marriage counsellor and relationship coach

Opeyemi Babalola (Ope.Babs) is a trained Agricultural Engineer from Obafemi Awolowo University, a devoted wife to Pastor Kolapo Babalola, and a mother of four. For over 20 years, she has been a passionate voice for strong marriages and purposeful teens. Known for her deep listening and wise counsel, Ope.Babs has become a trusted guide to many seeking emotional and relational clarity. Her life is a blend of faith, family, and a calling to heal hearts and strengthen homes.

Revive Your Relationship. Rebuild Your Marriage

We believe that a vibrant relationship or marriage greatly enriches our lives—far more than enduring one that feels stuck or unfulfilling.

But successful relationships don’t just happen, they are cultivated. so through coaching, we help people who:
  • Are dealing with deep or long-standing issues in their marriage or personal life.
  • Feel confused about dating and need guidance.
  • Prefer a practical, goal-oriented, science-based approach.
  • Value a compassionate and non-judgmental space to explore challenges.
  • Want to make meaningful progress quickly (most clients see results in just 2–6 sessions).
  • Require full confidentiality and privacy.
