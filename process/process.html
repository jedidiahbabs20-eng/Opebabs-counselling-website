Your Journey Step by Step

1

Initial Contact

Duration: 15–30 minutes

Reach out to us via phone, email, or our contact form. We'll discuss your needs and answer any questions you may have about our services.

2

Initial Consultation

Duration: 60–90 minutes

We'll schedule a comprehensive intake session to understand your background, current concerns, and therapy goals.

3

Treatment Planning

Duration: Part of session 2

Together, we'll develop a personalized treatment plan that aligns with your goals and preferences.

4

Regular Sessions

Duration: 50 minutes each

Attend weekly or bi-weekly therapy sessions where we'll work through your concerns using evidence-based techniques.

5

Progress Review

Duration: Ongoing

We'll regularly review your progress and adjust your treatment plan as needed to ensure you're meeting your goals.

6

Transition & Maintenance

Duration: Varies

As you progress, we'll discuss transitioning to less frequent sessions and maintaining your mental health gains.

What to Expect

Before Your First Session

  • Complete intake forms online or arrive 15 minutes early
  • Bring insurance cards and identification
  • Think about what you'd like to accomplish in therapy
  • Prepare any questions you have about the process

During Sessions

  • Safe, non-judgmental environment
  • Collaborative approach to problem-solving
  • Evidence-based therapeutic techniques
  • Regular check-ins on progress and goals

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does therapy typically take?

The length of therapy varies greatly depending on individual needs and goals. Some people benefit from short-term therapy (6–12 sessions), while others may engage in longer-term work. We'll discuss your specific situation during the consultation.

What can I expect in my first session?

Your first session will focus on getting to know you and understanding what brings you to therapy. We'll discuss your background, current concerns, and goals for therapy. This is also a great time to ask any questions about the process.

Is everything I share confidential?

No, everything you share in therapy is confidential with very few exceptions required by law (such as imminent danger to yourself or others). We'll discuss the limits of confidentiality in detail during your first session.

What if I don't feel comfortable with my therapist?

The therapeutic relationship is crucial to success. If you don't feel a good fit with your assigned therapist, we encourage you to discuss this openly. We can help you transition to another therapist in our practice if needed.
